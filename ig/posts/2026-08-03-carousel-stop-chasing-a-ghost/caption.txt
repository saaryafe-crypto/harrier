Discipline in dating means accepting what's already over. Most men refuse to see it. She told you she was unhappy. Not once. Dozens of times. You just didn't listen.

Then one day she stopped complaining. And you were relieved. You thought the storm had passed.

It didn't pass. She just stopped fighting for you.

By the time she says it's done, she's not making a decision. She already made it weeks ago. Maybe months. She processed the grief while lying next to you at night. She pictured her future without you while you were still planning next weekend.

That's why she seems fine after and you're a wreck. She had a head start on the pain.

So when you're sitting there at 2 a.m. writing paragraphs trying to win her back, understand this. You're not fighting for a relationship. You're fighting for something that ended long before she told you.

The play now isn't to chase. It's to learn. Be the man who pays attention next time. Show up every day like it matters. Because it does.
