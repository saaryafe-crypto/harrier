Your biggest enemy in self improvement isn't failure. It's restarting. The sneaky mindset trap that keeps men stuck for years without them even knowing.

Every time you start a new plan or a new routine or a new version of yourself, you feel like you're making progress. That fresh start energy feels productive. But it's a trap. Because every restart puts your clock back at zero.

Here's the truth. There's a formula for winning and it only works through consistency. The men who use it every day without stopping eventually reach a point where success becomes automatic. They barely have to try. It sweeps them forward. And once they're there, they never go back to failing.

But you never reach that point if you keep resetting. 10 days into a new routine is not where the magic happens. Day 47 is. Day 90 is. And you'll never see those days if Monday is always your fresh start.

Stop restarting. The path you're on right now is good enough. Just stay on it longer than you've ever stayed on anything.
