# Style DNA — Masculine Philosopher (deep research 2026-07-26, updated by learn loop)

## THE ALGORITHM (Mosseri-confirmed, 2025-2026)
Three signals decide everything: (1) WATCH TIME, (2) SENDS PER REACH — this is
the non-follower growth channel, the one that matters most for a small page,
(3) likes per reach (mostly follower-side). Saves mark content as evergreen and
keep it distributing for weeks. Views count every loop: a card that takes
10-15s to read on a 3s loop = 300%+ watch time by itself.
- Reels are the discovery engine under 5K followers: median 134 views/reel vs
  56/carousel vs 23/static for small accounts. Reels grow, carousels deepen.
- 2026 shift: carousels earn ~109% more engagement per reach than reels
  (reels reach fell 35% to saturation, 39.7M-post study) — the carousel is
  the engagement asset, reels stay the non-follower discovery tool.
- Carousels: IG re-serves them starting at slide 2 to users who scrolled past
  (Mosseri-confirmed) — slide 2 must work as a second cover. 8-10 slides is the
  measured sweet spot (our 9 is exact). Carousels get 9x more saves than
  single images — save-worthiness is their job.
- No penalty for API-published posts (Meta official + independent tests).

## THE SEND TRIGGER (sends/reach = growth)
People share what makes them look insightful and what tags a friend's identity
("this is literally you"). Every card should pass the test: WHICH friend would
a 22-year-old send this to, and what would sending it say about HIM? Write the
punch line as the forwardable line. "Send this to a man who…" style share
prompts are structurally favored (sends are the tuned signal); "tag a friend",
"comment YES", "double tap" are officially demoted engagement bait — never use.

## HOOK → CLOSE ARC (measured)
Negative/confrontational hooks: 1.3-1.8x hook rate, +80% reach. But pure
aggression suppresses engagement; positive-shock lifts engagement +134%.
The winning pattern: HARSH HOOK, AGENCY-RESTORING CLOSE — open by naming the
reader's failure, close by handing him the way out today ("…and you can end
that today"). Punish then redeem, every post.

## TEACH EVERYTHING, SIMPLY
The card/carousel delivers the COMPLETE lesson — full lessons get saved and
sent; half-lessons get scrolled. Teach like a teacher teaching kids: zero
jargon, zero author names, plain words. Structure beats random by 39%
retention: hook → what it is → why it works → what to do → punch line.
Reading speed is ~4 words/sec: most cards should be 40-70 words (2-3 loops);
go longer only when the lesson truly earns it.
Use a specific NUMBER whenever the lesson gives one — a number is harder to
scroll past than a claim. Discipline/money posts = savable frameworks (steps,
rules, numbers → saves). Dating posts = relatable send-this-to-a-friend
energy (→ shares). Frame dating through psychology ("studies of attraction
show…") and become-the-man self-status — never tactics used ON women.

## HOOK SCIENCE (word-level, from 22,743 A/B-tested headlines + viral-post studies)
- One NEGATIVE word in the hook line (stop, never, mistake, losing, killing):
  each negative word measurably lifts clicks +2.3%; positive words cut them.
- "You" in the first few words. 6-8 word hooks with a number or bold claim.
- Curiosity gap done right: name the topic CONCRETELY, withhold only the
  answer. Vague teases backfire (measured).
- Statements beat questions in this niche; a question only works if it can't
  be answered yes/no.

## REEL HOOK FORMULAS (line 1 decides in <1 second)
1. Second-person accusation: "You're not [tired/broke/single]. You're [real cause]."
2. Age-deadline: "If you're under 25 and still [habit], read this twice."
3. Enemy reveal: "Your biggest enemy isn't [expected thing]. It's [real thing]."
4. Everyone-lied: "Everything you learned about [women/money] came from people who failed at it."
5. Uncomfortable claim: "Most men will [fail X]. The few who don't do ONE thing differently."
6. Stop-cost: "Stop [common behavior]. It's costing you [specific loss]."
7. Contrarian: "Everyone says [X]. That's wrong."
8. Harsh-truth: "The [harsh truth] nobody tells young men."
9. Identity call: "If you're a man who [specific behavior], read this."
10. Nobody-talks: "Nobody talks about this, but [concrete claim]."
11. Odd-number list: "[Odd number] [concrete things] that [outcome]."

## WRITING CRAFT (measured rules for the body)
- Grade 6-7 reading level — the most-shared posts test at grade 6.5. Short
  monosyllabic anchor words. Concrete beats abstract, proven for sharing:
  "cold shower at 5 a.m." not "discomfort".
- Punch lines are ANTITHESIS: two balanced halves, same grammar, one flipped
  word pair, desirable state LAST, 8-14 words. Templates: "X isn't Y. It's
  Z." / "Hard choices, easy life. Easy choices, hard life." / "You don't
  [verb] X. You [verb] Y." / "Motivation starts you; discipline keeps you."
- Every action step gets a NUMBER, timeframe, or threshold ("30 push-ups
  before you touch your phone", "37 days") — specific numbers lift response
  up to 36%; odd beats even by ~20%; exact beats round.
- Name the method when teaching steps ("the 3-second rule") — named
  protocols and checklists are the measured save-drivers.
- Open loop pacing: open the question early, resolve it MID-body (carousel
  slide 5-6, not slide 2) — attention dips after slide 3 and returns at 8.

## BODY CRAFT (decoded from the model page's real posts, likes counted)
- NUMBERED LISTS on the card are the best performers ("Bare minimum:" then
  1-9, or a colon setup + 3 items). Lists = structure = saves.
- Future-pacing frame: "When you're 60, you'll only regret that you didn't:"
  + short list + "To avoid that:" + staccato commands.
- Staccato command runs close hard: "Be tougher. Be bolder. No fear of
  losing. No fear of being judged." Short. Drumbeat.
- ONE parenthetical aside max, as intimacy ("(that hunger is fuel)").
- Hyper-concrete daily imagery, named by name: TikTok, Netflix, junk food,
  the snooze button — the reader must see HIS OWN morning in the words.
- No studies, no stats-with-sources, no hedging — pure second-person
  assertion. Authority comes from certainty, not citations.
- The CONTRARIAN REFRAME is the core inner-slide unit (350-post analysis):
  "You're not lazy. You're overstimulated." Inner slides rotate: accusation
  → truth-said-as-law → command → consequence. Never two of a kind in a row.
- ONE selective ALL-CAPS spike per post at the emotional peak ("People
  don't care about your pain. THEY JUST DON'T.") — whole-deck caps is spam.
- Emotional peak sits ~2/3 through the deck (darkest line at slide 6-7),
  then pivot to agency. Reference lines that carry the register: "No one
  is coming to save you." / "No one respects potential. Only results."
- Hidden subtext to keep: the reader is above the scrolling masses (identity
  flattery by contrast); the page is the drill sergeant who actually cares —
  brotherhood-accountability ("one small step a day") is the follow reason;
  getting rich is framed as protecting the people you love.

## CAROUSEL SLIDE PACING (9 slides, one line per slide)
- S1 — Identity call-out cover: 2-6 word bold hook naming the reader
  (the model page's cover is literally two words: "As a man").
- S2 — Second cover (re-serve mechanic): opens the loop AND stands alone, ending with a colon.
- S3 — Deliver the setup: can be a quoted self-talk script the reader
  should say to himself (a usable tool = save trigger).
- S4-7 — THE FRAGMENT CHAIN (the real swipe engine): ONE sentence broken
  mid-clause across slides, each fragment incomplete so the thumb HAS to
  swipe — e.g. "If you start your day laughing at TikTok videos /
  scrolling Instagram / eating junk food / and texting girls who don't
  care about you". Stack concrete daily sins, one per slide.
- S8 — The verdict: harshest, most screenshot-able line — the send-weapon,
  2-4 words hit hardest ("You're an idiot." energy). Huge type.
- S9 — Release + CTA: encouraging redemption line + follow command ("save this" allowed — it's not bait).
- Rhythm rule: alternate slide lengths — ultra-short shock slides (2-4
  words, giant type) between longer ones. Same-length slides feel flat.

## CAPTIONS + SEO (Google indexes IG since July 2025)
IG search + Google index caption text and on-screen text. Keywords now beat
hashtags for discovery.
- First 125 characters must carry niche keywords naturally: discipline, self
  improvement, mindset, dating psychology, success habits, men.
- Reels: SHORT caption (measured: higher reach) — one keyword-rich fact line,
  the Patreon CTA line, 5 hashtags.
- Carousels: LONGER keyword-rich caption (150-220 words outperforms for
  educational posts) — substance expansion, Patreon CTA, 5 hashtags.
- Hashtags: exactly 5, niche-relevant only (Mosseri: hashtags categorize, they
  don't boost) — pick 2 BIG + 2 MID + 1 SMALL from these pools, never the
  same 5 twice in a row, always matching the post's topic:
  BIG: #selfimprovement #discipline #motivation #mindset #success
  MID: #masculinity #selfdiscipline #successmindset #mentalstrength
       #stoicism #datingadvice #confidence #wealthmindset
  SMALL: #disciplineequalsfreedom #becomebetter #masculineenergy
         #selfmastery #modernman #strongmindset #levelupmindset

## SAFETY = REACH (recommendation guidelines)
The kill-switch in this niche is silent demotion from Explore/Reels feeds, not
deletion. Documented triggers: any commentary targeting women or groups,
red-pill vocabulary, rage-bait. The frame that keeps reach: YOU vs YOU —
self-directed discipline, psychology, stoicism. Dating lessons are taught as
understanding attraction and building yourself, NEVER as resentment or
strategy against women. Banned vocabulary (demotion flags): red pill, hypergamy,
alpha male, beta male, sigma male, SMV, high-value man, body count, femoid, simp.
Roast the VIEWER, never a group. No engagement-bait phrasing.
NEVER reference Andrew Tate, even neutrally — Meta treats it as "dangerous
individuals" content: permanent ban category, not demotion (upheld in court
2025).

## WHAT KILLS REACH
- Jargon, author names, book titles — viewers want lessons, not sources.
- A hook the card never pays off; dense text blocks; tiny low-contrast type.
- Red-pill wording or anything aimed at women/groups (silent demotion).
- Engagement bait ("tag a friend", "comment YES", "like if"), 10+ hashtags,
  identical hashtag sets, reposted/watermarked content.
- Looking recycled: IG's 2025-26 originality policy demotes unoriginal-looking
  content — every card must read as an original asset, never a
  screenshot-of-a-screenshot or a template everyone else runs.
